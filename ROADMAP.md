# SalahPath product roadmap

## v3 current focus

- German and Turkish first-class UI
- GPS prayer times and Qibla
- Fard, Sunnah and Witr overview
- Separate male/female prayer-learning profiles
- Child-friendly Muslim prayer illustrations
- Wudu, Dhikr and Tasbih
- Quran reader with Arabic + DE/TR translation
- Human Quran recitation
- Human prayer-learning audio

## Next improvements

1. Quran bookmarks, last-read position and reading goals.
2. Downloadable Quran audio for offline listening with storage controls.
3. Memorisation mode with verse repeat and playback speed.
4. Morning/evening adhkar with source references and completion tracking.
5. Prayer tracker and optional make-up prayer log without social pressure.
6. Home-screen and lock-screen widgets for next prayer.
7. Apple Watch companion and complications later.
8. Mosque/Jumu'ah local times as an optional layer separate from calculated prayer start times.
9. Accessibility: Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, high contrast and reduced motion.
10. Content review: every prayer/dua item should carry source, school/context and review status.
11. Licensing review before public distribution for external Quran/dua audio and translations.
12. Offline-first cache with clear source/version metadata.
13. Tests for DST, travel, high latitudes, calculation methods and notification rollover.
14. Privacy-first analytics only if explicitly enabled; no precise-location history by default.

## Content principles

- Do not present one madhhab-specific detail as universal.
- Distinguish prayer start time from mosque iqamah/jama'ah time.
- Preserve vetted Quran translations; do not machine-retranslate them.
- Prefer documented human recitation sources and explicit distribution rights.
